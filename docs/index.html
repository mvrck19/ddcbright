Screen brightness, actually controlled.

What it does

Everything the tray icon should have done from the start.

01 · FLYOUT

Every monitor, one popup

No picker, no dropdown — every connected display gets its own row and slider, visible at once. Link them to move together, or leave them independent.

02 · SCHEDULE

Fades, not jump-cuts

Set a day and night target. Instant mode snaps at the scheduled time like before; Gradual eases over 15–120 minutes so you never notice it happening.

03 · AMBIENT

Watches the room, not the clock

Samples your webcam every 30 seconds and estimates ambient light instead of following a fixed schedule. Never dims below 10%.

04 · SCROLL

Wheel-to-adjust, everywhere

Scroll over any slider, or the tray icon itself, with no flyout open at all. A small readout confirms the level as you go.

05 · NATIVE

Not another Electron tray icon

Real Mica/Acrylic on Windows, matched to your accent color and theme.

06 · QUIET

Starts itself, stays out of the way

Registers to launch at login on first run via a Registry entry. Lives in the tray until you need it.
